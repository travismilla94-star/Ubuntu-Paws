About Ubuntu Paws Animal Rescue

Our history

Ubuntu Paws Animal Rescue was founded in 2019 by a small group of animal-welfare volunteers who began by fostering abandoned animals from their own homes. As demand grew, the group established a small shelter in Johannesburg and built a network of foster carers to extend its reach across Gauteng. Since then, the organisation has rehomed over 400 animals. See the animals currently looking for homes.

Our mission

To reduce animal suffering in Johannesburg through rescue, rehabilitation and community education.

Our vision

A community where no companion animal is homeless or neglected.

Questions about our work? Contact the team any time.

Ways you can help

Adopt

Give a rescued dog, cat or rabbit a permanent, loving home. Every animal is vaccinated, sterilised and health-checked before adoption.

Foster

Open your home to an animal for a few weeks while they recover and learn to trust people. We provide food, supplies and vet care.

Volunteer

Walk dogs, help at the shelter or support weekend adoption events. A few hours a month makes a real difference.
